Privacy policy

Estos: Tabata & Interval Timer. Effective 2026-10-26.

The developer of Estos does not collect any data from the app. Your timers and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your saved timers: names, interval lengths, repetitions, sets and exercise names. They live in one file in the app's storage. The Estos home-screen widget reads the same file on the same device.
  • Whether Premium is active, so the app and the widget work without a connection.
  • A few counters that decide when the app shows a prompt: how many workouts you finished, when you first opened the app, when it last asked for a rating, and which finish message it showed last.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that does any of these.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. The only connection it uses is Apple's purchase service, described next.

Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The app does not receive your payment details. It asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns Premium, and it uses the answer to unlock Premium features. Apple's privacy policy covers that part.

Files you import and share

When you import a timer file, the app reads the file you pick on your device and adds the timers to your library. Nothing is uploaded.

When you share a timer, the app writes a timer file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The file holds the timer's name, interval lengths, repetitions, sets and exercise names. It goes only where you send it. The developer of Estos does not see it. The person or service you send it to handles it under their own rules.

Audio, haptics and screen

During a workout the app plays its own sound cues, which can continue while the screen is locked. It uses haptic feedback and keeps the screen awake. None of this records or sends anything.

Links that leave the app

The Review row in Settings opens the app's page in the App Store. The Terms of Use link on the Premium screen opens Apple's standard terms. The Privacy Policy link opens this page in your browser. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives your request when you open it, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The app itself does not contact GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date changes with it.
