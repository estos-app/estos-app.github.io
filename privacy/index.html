Privacy policy

Estos collects no app data and has no ads or tracking.

On your iPhone
Your timers and settings stay on your device. The app keeps a record of each workout you do from version 1.2 on: when it started and ended, which timer it was, how many repetitions and sets you completed, and how long each kind of interval and each named exercise ran. Deleting a timer keeps its records. Deleting the app deletes this data.
Purchases
Apple processes Premium payments under its privacy policy. Estos does not receive your payment details.
Apple Health
If you have Premium and connect Apple Health in Settings, the app saves each workout to Apple Health when it ends. A workout you end before its first work interval is not saved. The app saves the kind of workout, the timer's name, when the workout started and ended, and when you paused it. It reads nothing from Apple Health. Apple Health keeps these workouts under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer of Estos never receives them. You turn saving on or off in Apple Health. Workouts the app saved stay there until you delete them.
Sharing
The developer shares no app data with third parties.
