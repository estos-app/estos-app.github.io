Questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The Restore Purchases button on the Premium screen does the same. The app checks your Apple Account for a Premium purchase. If you bought Premium with another Apple Account, sign in to that account in the App Store and try again.
What does Premium include?
Unlimited saved timers, sharing a timer as a file, and a home-screen widget that starts a timer in one tap. Premium comes as an annual subscription or as a one-time Lifetime purchase.
How do I cancel the annual subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Estos. Apple manages subscriptions, so the app has no cancel button. Premium stays on until the end of the year you paid for.
Why can I save only 2 timers?
The free version holds 2 saved timers. Creating or duplicating a third opens the Premium screen. You can delete a timer to make room, or get Premium for unlimited timers.
Can I import timers from other apps?
Yes, and importing is free. In Settings, tap Import Timers and pick a file. Estos opens timers shared from Estos, .tabata files and backups, and timers exported from Seconds. A file that is not a timer file is rejected and your library stays unchanged. Imported timers are added even if you already have more than 2.
